Privacy Policy

Blackout Guide does not collect personal information. There is no account, no analytics service, no advertising, and no server waiting for your notes.

Checklists, bookmarks, text size, and the red-light setting are stored on this device in local storage. They are not transmitted. Uninstalling the app, or using Erase saved data in Settings, removes them from the device.

Network

After the app is installed, the guides, search index, calculators, and tools are on the device. The app does not call an API, load remote fonts, or fetch images.

The installed phone app is the same library bundled inside the iOS build. Opening an article does not contact Blackout Guide or anyone else.

Camera and flashlight

On iOS, Morse code can use the phone flashlight. That uses the camera hardware only as a lamp. Blackout Guide does not take photos, record video, record audio, or store images. Nothing from the flashlight is sent anywhere.

The browser version flashes the screen instead, and does not ask for the camera.

Motion sensors

The compass, if you start it, reads the device orientation sensors to show a heading. The heading is shown on screen and is not stored or transmitted. You can use the sun and star instructions on that page without enabling the sensor.

Children

Blackout Guide is written for adults and older teenagers under adult guidance. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Purchases and tracking

There is no Blackout Guide account and no analytics. The app does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. It has no in-app purchase.

If you buy the app from the App Store, Apple processes that payment. Blackout Guide does not receive your payment details. The browser copy is the same library.

Contact

This page is the public privacy policy for Blackout Guide. Support is at /support/. Neither page asks for an account.
