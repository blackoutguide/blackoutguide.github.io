Support

Blackout Guide is an offline reference you install on your phone. There is no Blackout Guide account. The iPhone app and the browser copy include the same library.

The guides, search, checklists, and tools are stored on the device. Bookmarks and check marks stay there too. Erase saved data is in Settings.

Health and safety

Blackout Guide does not diagnose, treat, or cure any condition. Read the health and safety note in the app before you use the reference. If you can reach emergency services, contact them first.

Privacy

The app does not collect data, run analytics, or track you. It has no in-app purchase. The privacy policy is at /privacy/.
